A small utility for Mac

Calculate margins.
From your menu bar.

Enter cost, revenue, or gross margin.
Fill in any two and MarginMenu calculates the third.

Download for Mac

macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Two values in. One calculated.

Start with the numbers you know. Calculate cost, revenue, or margin without rearranging a formula.

Adjust the numbers.

Use the sliders to explore changes. Pin a value to keep it fixed while adjusting another.

Keep the precision.

See whole numbers at a glance. Decimals appear while editing and are preserved when you copy a value.

Installing MarginMenu

Signed and notarized for macOS.

  1. Open the downloaded disk image.
  2. Drag MarginMenu into Applications.
  3. Open the app, then click % in your menu bar.

Calculations stay on your Mac.
Update checks connect to GitHub. No account required.
