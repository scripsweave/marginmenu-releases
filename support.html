MarginMenu support

MarginMenu requires macOS 26 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Using the calculator

Enter any two of Cost, Revenue, and Margin to calculate the third. Gross margin is the difference between revenue and cost, divided by revenue. For example, cost 80 and revenue 100 produce a margin of 20%.

Open MarginMenu from the Dock or the percent icon in your menu bar. Use the ellipsis menu for help, appearance, and optional launch at login. Reset clears the current calculation and provides Undo.

Updates

For the GitHub edition, choose Check for Updates in the ellipsis menu. Mac App Store installations receive updates through the App Store.

Get help or report a problem

Open a support request on GitHub, or browse existing requests. Include your macOS version, MarginMenu version, and steps to reproduce the problem. A GitHub account is needed to post a request.

Requests are public. Do not include private financial information, passwords, or other sensitive details. You can also use this channel for questions about our privacy policy.
