Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Your calculations stay on your Mac

MarginMenu, developed by Jan van Rensburg, does not collect or transmit your calculations or personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Your completed calculation and preferences are saved locally on your Mac. Copying a result puts it on the macOS clipboard, which is managed by the operating system. Pasting reads the text you choose to paste.

Updates and external links

The edition downloaded from GitHub connects to GitHub to check for and download updates. GitHub receives ordinary web request information, such as your IP address, and records aggregate download counts. Your calculation is not included in these requests. Automatic checking and downloading can be turned off in the app’s Updates window.

The Mac App Store edition uses Apple’s distribution and update services and does not include the GitHub updater. Apple handles store activity under its own privacy policy.

Opening the website, this policy, or a support link uses your browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no added analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub and Apple operate their services under their respective GitHub privacy statement and Apple privacy policy.

Support

If you choose to contact us through GitHub, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and is used to respond to your request. Public issues are visible to everyone; avoid including private financial information. See the support page for help or privacy questions.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
